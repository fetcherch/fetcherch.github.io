How it works

Simple for drivers. Valuable for brands. A win-win strategy: AdCars.ch turns unused space on everyday cars into advertising space for brands. creating value for both drivers and businesses.

01

Drivers choose a brand

Drivers browse available campaigns and choose the brand they want to advertise.

02

Drivers order the ad film

AdCars.ch produces the printed, vehicle-suitable ad film and ships it directly to the driver with installation instructions.

03

Drivers create brand presence

Drivers earn their agreed monthly payout while the brand gains real-world presence wherever they drive, park and commute.

FAQ

Do I get paid every month?

Campaigns can be structured as monthly payouts. The exact rate and requirements are shown before you accept a campaign.

Who pays for the film?

The ad film and shipping are completely free for verified AdCars.ch users. Drivers never pay to participate in campaigns. AdCars.ch drivers are here to earn money, not spend it.

Can I choose the brand?

Yes. Drivers can browse eligible campaigns and select brands they are interested in, subject to availability.

Is the film removable?

The site is designed around removable perforated window film. Always use vehicle-rated materials and professional installation where appropriate.
